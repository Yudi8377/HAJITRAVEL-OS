# Android Pilgrim Platform

Standard: itinerary, documents, flight/hotel/transport, contacts, AI Companion and journey timeline.

Pro: consented location, contextual separation detection, geofencing, emergency, health signals, transport and permit monitoring.

Enterprise: Command Center, Journey Twin, Readiness Graph, AI Risk Engine, wearable integration, supplier monitoring, bus tracking, predictive analytics, family delegation, Consent Passport, regulatory intelligence and enterprise integrations.

Premium modules include Digital Pilgrim ID (QR/NFC/BLE/wallet), Offline Sanctuary, Trust Ledger, Journey Black Box and Elderly/Accessibility Mode.
