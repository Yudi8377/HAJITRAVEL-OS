# Acceptance Criteria

Identity: lifecycle, revocation, audit and fallback.
Safety: contextual separation, stale-location distinction, consent and escalation.
Health: explicit consent, scoped access, no diagnosis.
Operations: readiness dependencies and actionable travel exceptions.
Finance: source, approval, accounting trace and reconciliation.
Compliance: authoritative source, status/effective date and impact mapping.
AI: provenance, confidence, policy evaluation and human override.
Security: RLS, organization isolation, capability checks and audit tests.
UX: responsive, accessible, fast, clear error/loading/empty/offline states.
Release: E2E, smoke, observability and rollback plan.
